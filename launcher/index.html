Minecraft 26.3

World Generation

• The Mottled Forest is a new autumn biome that appears near cold regions. New trees grow here, along with red bushes, brown mushrooms and fallen leaves.

• Abandoned Camp is a new natural structure that can appear in many forest and plains biomes.

• New details, vegetation, fallen trunks and decorative elements make exploration more varied.

New Blocks

• A complete set of decorative wood blocks has been added, including stairs, slabs, signs, doors, fences, gates and trapdoors.

• Red Bush is a decorative plant with a new appearance.

• Wool Stairs and Slabs provide additional building options.

Gameplay

• Various improvements and bug fixes have been included in this update.
